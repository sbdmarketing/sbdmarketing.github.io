Search Console's API Now Splits Branded Queries: Defense Firm Notes

On November 16, 2026, Google added a branded query classification to the Search Analytics API, the programmatic side of the Search Console Performance report. The same filter had already appeared in the Search Console interface in November 2025. The difference now is that anyone pulling data through the API, or through a reporting tool built on it, can separate searches for a firm's own name from searches for the charges it handles.

For a criminal defense firm this matters more than it sounds. Branded and unbranded searches come from different people at different moments. Mixing them in one report hides the number most firms care about: how many strangers found the firm while looking for help with a charge.

What Google added to the API

According to Google's announcement and its API reference, the update adds a dimension called brandedClassification with two values, BRANDED and NON_BRANDED. You can group results by it or filter on it. A few limits come with it:

  • Data starts on February 21, 2026, so older periods cannot be split this way.
  • It works only for top-level properties with enough query and impression volume. Google does not define the threshold, and a property that falls short gets a response with no rows instead of an error.
  • A branded classification filter cannot be combined with a query filter in the same request.

The same release added a search type for multimodal searches such as Google Lens and Circle to Search, plus a way to filter on lists of countries or devices. Those matter less to most defense firms, though a practice near a border may find the country filter handy.

Why branded and unbranded traffic tell different stories

Someone who types a firm's name has already heard of it. A referral, a friend, a billboard, an AI assistant's answer or a past case put the name in their head. Those clicks say something about reputation and word of mouth. They say little about whether the website ranks for "DUI lawyer" in your county.

Unbranded searches are the ones a firm competes for: a charge plus a city, a question about bail, the name of a courthouse. When a firm's SEO is working, unbranded clicks to charge pages should climb over time. When branded searches are folded in, a busy month of referrals can make a weak SEO month look healthy. The reverse happens too.

Practical uses for a defense firm

A firm or its marketing team might use the split in these ways.

  1. Report unbranded clicks to charge pages separately each month. This is the cleanest view of whether new content and technical fixes are reaching people who never heard of you.
  2. Watch branded impressions as a rough signal of awareness. A slow rise can follow news coverage, more reviews or mentions in AI tools.
  3. Use it to read the current core update. Google's November 2026 core update began rolling out on November 10. If rankings moved, the unbranded series will show it more clearly than a blended total.

The second use connects to work covered elsewhere in the network on using branded search data to gauge how often AI tools name a firm. With the API change, that tracking is easier to automate.

Keep in mind that Google decides which queries count as branded. Misspellings of a lawyer's name, or a firm name that is also an ordinary word, may be sorted in ways you would not expect. Spot check the queries in the interface before you trust a trend.

What to ask your agency or developer

If someone else builds your reports, ask whether their dashboard has been updated to use the new dimension. Many reporting tools pull from the API, so the change may show up in outside tools over the coming weeks or months, depending on the vendor.

Ask also whether your property qualifies. Smaller solo practices may get empty results if their search volume is low. In that case the fallback is a manual filter on the lawyer's and firm's name variations, which has been the workaround for years and still works.

Reading the numbers calmly

Search data for defense firms is lumpy. Arrests cluster around holidays, weekends and big local events, and a single news story can spike branded searches for a week. Compare the same months year over year where you can, and treat a month or two of movement with caution. The split data only goes back to February 2026, so a true year-over-year comparison will not be possible until early 2027.

Questions about the branded query split

Do I need the API to see branded and unbranded queries?

No. The Search Console Performance report has offered a branded queries filter since late 2025 for eligible properties. The API change mostly matters for automated reports and third-party tools.

Why does my property return no branded data?

Google limits the classification to top-level properties with enough query and impression volume. If a site is new or small, the response may come back empty.

Does a rise in branded searches help rankings?

Google has not said that branded search volume is a ranking factor. Treat it as a measure of awareness, and judge SEO progress on the unbranded numbers.
